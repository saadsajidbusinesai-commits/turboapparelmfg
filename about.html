Our Story

Founded 2019, Sialkot

Turbo Apparel MFG started as a small custom order shop and grew into a full production facility handling everything from raw yarn to finished, packed garments. Seven years in, we still run every stage in-house — knitting, dyeing, cutting, printing, embroidery, QC and packing — because that's the only way to guarantee the consistency our clients depend on.

Today we manufacture for streetwear labels, uniform suppliers and sportswear brands across the globe, with a minimum order of just 50 pieces so growing brands can start small and scale with us.
